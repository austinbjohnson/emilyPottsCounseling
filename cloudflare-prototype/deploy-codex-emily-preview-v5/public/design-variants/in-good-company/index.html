Hi, I’m Emily.
I’m glad you’re here.

I offer a kind of therapy that invites your full humanity in.

I’m a Registered Clinical Counsellor. I work with people who feel overwhelmed, disconnected, or like they’re carrying too much—especially those who care deeply about the world and their place in it.

Before becoming a therapist, I spent years guiding groups in outdoor spaces, building trust and helping people reconnect with themselves and each other. That experience continues to shape how I show up: relational, grounded, and beside you.

There’s room for grief and confusion, for curiosity and laughter. You don’t need to show up polished or certain.

Come as you are.

A little more room
to be yourself.

A confidential, supportive space to explore your thoughts, feelings, and patterns, guided by your experience and your goals.

Individual counselling

For the things you’re carrying: anxiety, shame, burnout, grief, or a sense of disconnection. Together, we can make space to understand your experience and what matters to you.

Couples counselling

A space to explore your relationship, the patterns you find yourselves in, and how you connect with one another. Our work is collaborative and grounded in your lived experience.

We make sense of it, together.

What we carry doesn’t live in a vacuum. Our lives are shaped by relationships, culture, and the systems around us.

My approach is relational and trauma-informed, drawing on narrative work, feminist theory, and nature-based therapy. Our sessions are shaped by you—your insight, your pace, and the life you’re living.

You can bring your humour, your questions, and the parts that feel difficult to put into words.

Before we meet.

What happens in a consultation?

A free 20-minute consultation is a chance to connect, ask questions, and see if working together feels like a fit. There’s no pressure to have it all figured out.

Where can we meet?

I offer in-person counselling in North Vancouver and online counselling throughout British Columbia.

How do I book?

You can book a free consultation or book a session through my online booking page. If you have a question first, email hello@emilypotts.ca.

A quieter place to start

One to one.

Individual counselling offers space to slow down and explore what you’re carrying, at a pace that works for you. You don’t need to arrive with the right words or a clear plan.

We can talk about anxiety, burnout, grief, identity, or feeling disconnected. Emily’s work is relational and collaborative, shaped around your lived experience and what matters to you.

Practical details
In person in North Vancouver or online throughout British Columbia. The first step can be a free 20-minute consultation.

Making room for two

Side by side.

Couples counselling makes space to notice the patterns you find yourselves in, how you communicate, and what connection could look like for both of you.

The work is collaborative. You can bring different perspectives and take time to understand what each person needs from the conversation.

Practical details
Sessions are available in person in North Vancouver and online across BC. A free 20-minute consultation is a chance to ask questions and see whether working together feels like a fit.

A change of setting

Out in the open.

For some people, moving while talking feels more comfortable than sitting across from one another. Walk-and-talk sessions bring counselling outdoors, where the pace and surroundings can become part of the conversation.

It’s one option among several. We can discuss what feels workable for you before deciding how to meet.

Practical details
Walk-and-talk sessions are individual sessions in North Vancouver, weather permitting. Online sessions are also available throughout BC.

Emily Potts is a Registered Clinical Counsellor (RCC). Her approach is relational and trauma-informed, drawing on narrative work, feminist theory, and nature-based therapy.

Before becoming a therapist, Emily spent years guiding groups in outdoor spaces. That experience continues to shape the way she works: with curiosity, collaboration, and respect for each person’s pace.

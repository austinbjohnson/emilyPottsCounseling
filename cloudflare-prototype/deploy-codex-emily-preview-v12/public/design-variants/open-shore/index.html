Hi, I’m Emily.
I’m glad you’re here.

I show up as a whole person — warm, present, and curious — because I think that’s part of what makes it safe enough for you to do the same.

Before I was a therapist, I spent years guiding people through the backcountry. Experiencing the impact nature has on our mental health was what led me to this work.

In our sessions, humour and pain often sit side by side. All of you is welcome here.

Individual counselling

Couples counselling

Room for two people to settle, reconnect, and explore what’s happening in your relationship.

Walk & talk therapy

Therapy in motion, outdoors. For some people, walking in nature may feel more comfortable than sitting in an office.

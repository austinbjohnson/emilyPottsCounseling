Before becoming a counsellor, I spent years guiding people outdoors. Building trust and connecting with the world around us continue to shape my practice.

I draw on relational, narrative, feminist, and nature-based approaches. Together, we can explore what feels relevant to your life.

You can bring any questions you have, or share a little about what brings you here. You don’t need to have everything worked out beforehand.

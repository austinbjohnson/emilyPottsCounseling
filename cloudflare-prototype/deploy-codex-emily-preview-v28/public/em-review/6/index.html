A little more about me

You won't find a blank, nodding stranger across the room from me. I show up as a whole person — warm, present, and curious — because I think that's part of what makes it safe enough for you to do the same.

Before I was a therapist, I spent years guiding people through the backcountry, days at a time, far from the noise. Experiencing the impact nature has on our mental health was what led me to this work. I’d watch people soften, open up, laugh, and remember they were stronger and more connected than they'd given themselves credit for.

That settling is the heart of what led me to become a therapist and how I still work. My hope is to create a space where you can slow down enough to feel safe, and your own capacity can come back online.

In our sessions, humour and pain often sit side by side. All of you is welcome here: the witty and the wounded, the confident and the confused.

I draw on trauma-informed, relational, and nature-based approaches, but the method matters less than what happens between us.

Change tends to come when you feel genuinely met: not judged, not managed, just accompanied. My job is to help create the conditions where that's possible. The rest unfolds at your pace.

Meet Emily

A person before a practice.

Emily is a Registered Clinical Counsellor based in North Vancouver.

Her background includes years guiding people through the backcountry, before her counselling training.

Her public About page describes a relational, narrative, feminist, and nature-informed approach, alongside her professional qualifications and training.

Ways to meet

There is more than one setting.

Individual and relationship counselling, and Walk & Talk sessions. In-person sessions are in North Vancouver; virtual sessions are offered across BC. Walk & Talk is weather permitting.

  • Individual adults
  • Couples & relationships
  • Walk & Talk
  • Virtual across BC

50-minute fees: $174 for individual or Walk & Talk; $227 for couples. Full fees and 75-minute options are on Emily’s service page.

Her listed 75-minute fees are $262 for individual or Walk & Talk and $339 for couples.

Emily’s current services page says Walk & Talk is offered year-round when weather permits, with a different plan made together when it is not safe to meet outdoors. Check her full page for the latest arrangements and fees.

Getting started

Emily is a Registered Clinical Counsellor based in North Vancouver. She offers a free 20-minute consultation, and virtual counselling across British Columbia.

Who is Emily?

Emily’s public About page describes her work as relational, nature-informed and collaborative. Her qualifications include a master’s degree in Counselling Psychology.

Read Emily’s complete About page

How can we meet?

Her services include individual adults, couples and relationships, and Walk & Talk. In-person services are in North Vancouver; virtual sessions are available across BC.

What are the fees?

Individual and Walk & Talk: $174 for 50 minutes or $262 for 75 minutes. Couples: $227 for 50 minutes or $339 for 75 minutes. Check Emily’s current page before booking.

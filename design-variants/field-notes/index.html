Alongside is a good place to start.

Before becoming a therapist, I spent years guiding groups outdoors: building trust and helping people reconnect with themselves and each other.

That experience shapes how I work today. My practice is relational, trauma-informed, and grounded in feminist care. Your experiences and your goals help shape our work.

There’s room here for grief and confusion, curiosity and laughter. You can arrive as you are.

Individual counselling

One-to-one support for anxiety, burnout, grief, identity exploration, and emotional disconnection. In person in North Vancouver or online across BC.

Couples & relationships

A place to explore conflict, patterns, and connection together. Support shaped around your relationship and what you hope to understand.

Walk & talk
